# Genre and art-direction guide

Use semantic analysis first and keywords only as supporting evidence. For hybrid books, choose one primary market shelf and borrow at most one secondary style cue.

| Genre | Signals | Art direction | Typography | Palette tendencies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sci-fi | future, AI, space, cybernetics, robots, interstellar travel | cinematic futuristic sci-fi with controlled neon and a bold technological symbol | bold geometric sans or futuristic display | navy, black, cyan, violet, electric accents |
| fantasy | magic, dragons, kingdoms, knights, mythology | epic fantasy cinematic artwork with atmospheric depth and one iconic artifact or character | cinematic serif; restrained ornament | deep blue, emerald, gold, ember |
| thriller | mystery, murder, secrets, crime, disappearance | dark psychological thriller with cropped clues, negative space, and tension | condensed bold sans or sharp serif | charcoal, off-white, red accent |
| romance | love, youth, encounter, relationship | soft emotional romantic imagery; intimate gesture or symbolic place, not generic stock-photo posing | elegant soft serif | blush, cream, dusk blue, warm gold |
| business | wealth, entrepreneurship, leadership, investing | premium minimal cover with one intelligent visual metaphor and ample space | clean modern sans | black, white, navy, restrained metallic accent |
| horror | uncanny, demons, terror, death, haunting | dark atmospheric horror with implication rather than gore | distressed or severe cinematic display, used sparingly | black, sickly green, ash, blood-red accent |
| literary | memory, family, identity, social observation, interior life | editorial, symbolic, artful restraint; tactile photographic or painterly treatment | sophisticated serif | muted, seasonal, paper-like tones |
| historical | named era, dynasty, war, archival setting | period-aware cinematic or archival-inspired composition; avoid anachronisms | classical serif appropriate to language | sepia, oxidized metal, textile colors |
| memoir | first-person life story, recovery, journey, testimony | authentic portrait or resonant personal object with editorial restraint | humanist serif or clean sans | natural, warm, documentary tones |
| self-help | habits, psychology, growth, productivity | bold, simple visual metaphor optimized for thumbnails | friendly modern sans | bright controlled accent on clean field |

## Selection rules

- Infer audience and shelf before style. A business memoir should not look like an investment manual unless the synopsis supports it.
- Avoid direct imitation of a living artist or a copyrighted franchise. Describe visual qualities instead.
- Align with current market conventions only at a category level; do not copy a specific existing cover.
- Use culturally and historically accurate details when the story names a real place or period.
- Prefer a single memorable symbol, silhouette, face, or environment. Do not illustrate every plot point.
