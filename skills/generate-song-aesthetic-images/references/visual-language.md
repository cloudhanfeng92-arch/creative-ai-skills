# Song-inspired visual language

Use this as a stable visual grammar, then adapt the subject matter to the user's request. The target is not generic “ancient China,” palace costume drama, fantasy, or ink painting. It is a believable cinematic still of quiet Chinese everyday life filtered through restrained Song aesthetics.

## STYLE_LOCK

Preserve these traits across a series:

- **Emotional register:** calm, intimate, observant, slow, and unperformed; beauty comes from ordinary work, weather, and companionship rather than pageantry.
- **Image character:** photorealistic cinematic still with documentary credibility; clear material detail, natural skin texture, subtle natural facial variation, restrained grading, and no beauty-advertising polish.
- **Spatial character:** large quiet areas, asymmetric balance, foreground occlusion or framing, and legible foreground/midground/background layers. Let architecture, foliage, water, or shadow hold part of the frame.
- **Architecture and landscape:** limewashed walls, blue-gray stone, dark aged timber, bamboo blinds and trellises, narrow windows, modest courtyards, creek edges, lotus ponds, simple wooden boats, distant tiled roofs, and cultivated gardens. Use only what the theme needs.
- **Materials:** weighted cotton-linen, rough clay, woven bamboo, weathered wood, stone, paper, water, leaves, hemp, or unglazed ceramic. Describe the material behavior, not just its name.
- **Palette:** rice white, moon white, pale moss, bamboo green, smoky blue, light lotus pink, tea brown, ink brown, and warm gray. Keep saturation low and reserve bright color for one small narrative accent.
- **People:** natural posture, task-focused gaze, light or no visible makeup, believable age, distinct faces, and simple Song-inspired cotton-linen layers. Avoid theatrical robes, luxury embroidery, costume-shop styling, or modern tailoring.

Do not make all scenes use every item above. Repetition of the entire vocabulary produces formulaic pictures; preserve the grammar while choosing only the details that support the theme.

## Composition and camera

Choose the camera for the scene's job:

| Scene job | Useful default | Composition behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Environment or group activity | 50mm eye-level medium-wide | Keep the setting broad enough to explain the work and relationships. |
| Activity on water or near the ground | 40mm low water-level or low-angle medium-long | Anchor boat, bank, tool, and people in one readable spatial system. |
| Single-person quiet moment | 85mm three-quarter medium-close | Put the face off-center; preserve wall, window, shadow, or foliage as breathing room. |
| Interior craft or still life | 50–65mm doorway-framed medium view | Use a dark opening or foreground object as a frame and let surfaces reveal texture. |

Prefer asymmetric arcs, diagonals, or staggered depth over a straight lineup. A group should not read as a graduation photo. Near objects may be partially cropped, but faces, hands, and key tools must remain readable.

## Lighting grammar

Light must have a named source, direction, and job. Use one dominant source plus at most one or two motivated secondary effects.

- **Bamboo-blind light:** thin warm bands cross wall, sleeve, or part of the face; a pale wall or open courtyard provides cool fill.
- **Leaf-filtered daylight:** warm broken light touches cuffs, hair rims, hands, or produce; canopy shade keeps most of the scene soft and cool.
- **Water scene:** sky or foliage produces a cool green-blue reflection under chins and sleeves; low warm light may edge leaves or fabric without lighting every face equally.
- **Moonlit courtyard:** cool moonlight establishes wall, stone, and negative space; one oil lamp warms hands and the immediate work surface only.
- **Urban night:** paper lanterns, candlelit shop openings, and sparse warm window light pick out people or goods; damp stone may return small warm reflections while the street remains muted. Avoid neon, exaggerated cyan-orange grading, and every lantern glowing at the same intensity.
- **Rain or overcast:** broad cool light, darkened stone and timber, subdued highlights, and small warm interior accents.

Avoid flat global illumination, uniform face lighting, neon color contrast, excessive haze, blown highlights, and arbitrary god rays.

## Material behavior cues

- Linen folds should sag, crease, and bunch with visible fiber and weight.
- Wet cloth should darken, cling locally, pull downward, and shed water from its lowest folds.
- Water should ripple from the actual contact point; reflections should follow the surface plane.
- Clay and stone should be matte, irregular, and slightly worn rather than glossy resin.
- Bamboo and tools need coherent joints, handles, weave direction, and scale.
- Steam, dust, rain, and smoke should be sparse and motivated by an object in the scene.

## Theme adaptations

- **No-person landscape:** keep the same palette, material truth, quiet negative space, and motivated light; do not add a decorative woman.
- **Men, elders, or children:** preserve the requested demographics and give age-appropriate posture, work, clothing, and facial texture.
- **Urban Song-inspired life:** replace rural shorthand with restrained alleys, bridges, shops, paper screens, wood, tile, and street activity; retain the quiet observational camera.
- **Ceremony, festival, or affluent setting:** allow finer fabric, denser activity, and ceremonial objects to the degree requested; keep materials, light, posture, and color credible rather than turning the scene into palace spectacle.
- **Historically exact request:** separate verified facts from aesthetic invention and do not describe conjecture as authentic reconstruction.

## Anti-style boundary

Unless explicitly requested, exclude watercolor or ink-brush rendering, xianxia glow, wuxia action, palace spectacle, ornate crowns, anime features, fashion-editorial posing, commercial studio lighting, plastic skin, heavy makeup, modern objects, tutorial typography, captions, borders, and watermarks.
